Reference Letters

I am happy to support undergraduate and postgraduate students by providing academic reference letters where I am able to do so. Please read the conditions below before emailing your request, as they help me manage applications fairly and prepare references to an appropriate standard.

Before Making a Request

I aim to accommodate reference requests from students whenever my availability and the application timetable allow.

  • January to April I cannot accept reference requests between January and April. Outside this period, you are welcome to contact me by email. Please note, however, that even outside this period I cannot guarantee that I will be able to write a reference, given the high volume of requests and my other commitments.
  • Minimum notice Due to the number of requests I receive, I can consider only applications whose submission deadline is at least three weeks away when you contact me. This is necessary, as I cannot guarantee that a reference will be completed in time when less than three weeks' notice is provided.

Request a Reference Letter

Email Your Request Email your request outside the January–April restricted period and at least three weeks before the deadline.
