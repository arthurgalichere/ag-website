Booking an Individual Appointment

Dear Student,

I hope you are doing well.

To discuss module content, feedback, assessment preparation, or dissertation work, you can arrange an individual A&F appointment through my personal Microsoft booking page.

Book a Time with Arthur Galichère
  • View available appointments and reserve a suitable meeting time.
  • Appointments can usually be booked only up to one week in advance.

My booking page is set to UK time. Please ensure that the time zone in your Microsoft 365 or Outlook account is also set to UK time; otherwise, check the displayed time conversion carefully and plan accordingly.

The booking system usually works smoothly and has been used successfully by many students. Occasionally, however, a booking cannot be confirmed. This may happen if another student selects the same appointment at the same time, or if stored browser cookies interfere with the booking process.

If you experience a booking problem, try:

  1. Open the booking page in a private or Incognito browsing window.
  2. Choose the option to book as a guest instead of signing in with your Microsoft account.
  3. If the problem continues, try completing the booking from your phone.

I hope this information is helpful.

Best,
Arthur
