Economics

This section is being developed as a portal for economic data, visual analysis, forecasting tools, and interactive macroeconomic simulations. It will bring together empirical indicators and model-based resources designed for students, researchers, and anyone interested in understanding macroeconomic dynamics.

Work in progress. The individual resources are not yet available. Main Economic Indicators and the RBC Model Simulation and Forecasting page are planned as the first releases, with development expected to begin over the next one to two months. The remaining sections will be introduced gradually as the underlying data and modelling tools are completed.

Data and Economic Analysis

Dashboards and analytical resources for monitoring economic activity, financial markets, and the business cycle.

Main Economic Indicators

Next

Interactive time series for key indicators such as output, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, consumption, investment, and other measures of macroeconomic performance.

Real Business Cycles

Planned

Resources for identifying, measuring, and comparing expansions, recessions, cyclical comovement, volatility, and the behaviour of macroeconomic variables over the business cycle.

Aggregate Stock Market Indicators

Planned

Aggregate measures of stock-market performance, valuation, volatility, risk premia, and their relationship with real economic activity and financial conditions.

Economic Forecasting

Planned

Forecasting exercises, model comparisons, uncertainty intervals, and tools for evaluating predictions of output, inflation, labour-market conditions, and other economic variables.

Macroeconomic Models and Simulations

Interactive resources for exploring the mechanisms, simulations, and forecasting implications of modern macroeconomic models.

RBC Model Simulation and Forecasting

Next

An interactive Real Business Cycle environment for studying equilibrium dynamics, impulse responses, simulated time series, calibration, and model-based forecasts.

New Keynesian Model Simulation and Forecasting

Planned

Simulations of a New Keynesian framework featuring nominal rigidities, monetary-policy rules, macroeconomic shocks, impulse responses, and forecasting applications.

DSGE Modelling

Planned

A collection of dynamic stochastic general equilibrium models, ranging from foundational value and market models to richer macro-finance frameworks. This section will present model structures, solution methods, simulations, quantitative implementation, and applications across multiple dedicated subpages.

Cards will become active links as each resource is completed and published.
